Conga and Nintex both built their Salesforce presence around document generation: turning Salesforce data into contracts, proposals, and renewal notices. Neither product starts with data collection. That division of labor is more useful to understand than to fight, since most renewal and document workflows actually need both halves working together, not one replacing the other.
